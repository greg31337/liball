FAQ

Q1. What kind of SDKs do Nikon offer?

A1. We offer two types of SDKs:
1. A Camera Remote SDK to integrate remote control features for Nikon cameras into your application software.
2. An Image SDK to open RAW images (NEF and NRW files) taken with Nikon cameras in your application software.

Q2. Are there any limitations when using the SDKs?

A2. All limitations you need to follow (if any) are written in the license agreements presented when you download the SDKs.
Please read thoroughly when agreeing to the agreements to download the SDKs.

Q3. Are there any fees involved when using the SDKs?

A3. No, the use of SDK is free within the limit of license agreements.

Q4. Do you offer any technical support for the SDKs?

A4. No, we do not offer technical support for the SDKs.

Q5. Is there anywhere I can ask for assistance about the SDK?

A5. We do not have a contact point for technical inquiries regarding the SDK

Q6. When is the update for the SDK that's compatible with a new camera released?

A6. The SDKs are updated or added for a new camera sometime after the camera is released.
The SDK to remotely control cameras are added per camera
The SDK to open raw images (NEF and NRW files) are updated per camera.

Q7. Do you offer any notification service when there are any changes (added, updated, or deleted) made to the SDKs?

A7. No, we do not offer such service.

Q8. Do you plan to update the currently available SDKs for future operating systems?

A8. We cannot give you a clear plan for future releases.

Q9. Do you plan to fix the bugs existing in the currently available SDKs?

A9. We cannot give you a clear plan for future releases.

Q10. Is it possible that you stop distributing the SDKs?

A10. It is possible that we stop distributing SDKs for hardware we finished selling.

Q11. Which cameras are remotely controllable using the SDKs?

A11. The SDKs support all cameras that can be remotely controlled via NX Tether or Camera Control Pro 2.

Q12. Do the SDKs to remotely control cameras support controlling multiple cameras at the same time?

A12. No, the SDKs do not support multiple cameras at the same time.

Q13. Do the SDKs to remotely control cameras support controlling third party lenses and accessories?

A13. No, the SDKs only support cameras made by Nikon.

Q14. Do the SDKs to remotely control cameras support taking movies?

A14. Yes, it is possible to control the cameras while taking movies, although older model cameras may not be supported.
Please refer to the documents found inside the SDK archive files.

Q15. Are all NEF and NRW files supported by Image SDK?

A15. All raw images taken by Nikon made cameras are supported.
Please refer to the documents found inside the SDK archive files.
