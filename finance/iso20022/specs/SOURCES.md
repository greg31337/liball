## Integrity check

Run `find specs -type f -print0 | sort -z | xargs -0 shasum -a 256` and compare the ISO artifact hashes above before using them as a generation baseline. Research HTML/README snapshots are time-sensitive and should be refreshed deliberately, never implicitly during a build.
